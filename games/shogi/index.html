Shogi

Japanese Chess · 将棋

Japanese chess on a 9×9 board, where nothing you capture is lost for good: take an enemy piece and you can drop it back onto the board as your own.

  • 2 players
  • vs AI or a friend
  • Works offline
  • Free

How to play Shogi

  • Each side has twenty pieces: a king, a rook, a bishop, two gold and two silver generals, two knights, two lances and nine pawns.
  • Sente moves first.
  • Captured pieces go to your hand, and on a later turn you may drop one onto an empty square instead of moving.
  • Most pieces may promote when they move into, within or out of the last three ranks.
  • Checkmate the enemy king to win.
