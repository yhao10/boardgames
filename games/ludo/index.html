Ludo

The family race game

Roll the die, bring your four tokens out, run them round the board and get them all home before anyone else.

  • 2, 3 or 4 players
  • vs AI or a friend
  • Works offline
  • Free

How to play Ludo

  • Roll a six to bring a token out of your yard.
  • Move a token forward by the number you roll.
  • Land on an opponent's token to send it back to its yard; tokens on safe squares cannot be captured.
  • Get all four tokens home first to win.
