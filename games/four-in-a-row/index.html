Four in a Row

The drop-disc line game

Drop coloured discs into a grid seven columns wide and six rows high, and be the first to line up four.

  • 2 players
  • vs AI or a friend
  • Works offline
  • Free

How to play Four in a Row

  • Take turns dropping a disc into any column; it falls to the lowest empty space.
  • Four of your discs in a line, across, down or diagonally, wins.
  • If the grid fills up with no line of four, the game is drawn.
