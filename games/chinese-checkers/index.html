Chinese Checkers

Star Halma · 跳棋

Race your marbles across a six-pointed star. Hop over any marble, yours or an opponent's, and chain hops together to cross the board in a single turn.

  • 2, 3, 4 or 6 players
  • vs AI or a friend
  • Works offline
  • Free

How to play Chinese Checkers

  • Each player starts with ten marbles in one point of the star.
  • On your turn, step to a neighbouring empty hole, or hop over an adjacent marble into the empty hole beyond.
  • Hops can be chained in one turn, and nothing is ever captured.
  • The first player to move all ten marbles into the opposite point wins.
