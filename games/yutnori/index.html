Yutnori

Yut Nori · 윷놀이

Korea's traditional New Year game. Throw four yut sticks, race your markers around the board, and cut through the middle on the corner shortcuts.

  • 2 players
  • vs AI or a friend
  • Works offline
  • Free

How to play Yutnori

  • Throw four sticks. The result moves you one to five spaces: Do (1), Gae (2), Geol (3), Yut (4) or Mo (5).
  • Throwing Yut or Mo, or capturing an opponent's marker, earns another throw.
  • Stop on a corner and you can take the diagonal shortcut through the centre.
  • Markers that share a point travel together as a stack.
  • Land on an opponent to send their markers back to the start. Bring all four markers home first to win.
