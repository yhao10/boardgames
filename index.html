The Asian board games you grew up with

Most board game apps stop at Chess and Ludo. This one also brings the games played on kitchen tables, school desks and New Year floors across Asia: Chinese Chess and Japanese Shogi, Gomoku, the Animal Chess every child in China knows, the bluffing battles of Army Chess, the Flying Chess race, and Korea's Yutnori.

They are hard to find in one place, and harder to find with an opponent who knows the rules. Here each one has a computer player that does, and it plays without an internet connection.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. All twelve games, the Easy and Normal AI, and two-player games on one device are free and unlimited. Optional short ads add extra Undo and Load uses, and a one-time Premium purchase unlocks the Expert AI, unlimited Undo and Load, and removes all ads. There is no subscription.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every game and the AI run on your phone, so you can play on a plane, on the subway or anywhere without a signal. Only ads and purchases use the internet.

Can two people play on one phone?

Yes. Every game supports pass-and-play on one device. Ludo and Aeroplane Chess take up to four players and Chinese Checkers up to six, and any seat can be a person or the AI.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and no sign-in.

Which languages does it support?

Ten: English, 简体中文, 日本語, 한국어, Tiếng Việt, Bahasa Melayu, ไทย, ລາວ, Español, Deutsch.

Which devices does it run on?

Android 7.0 or newer, on phones and tablets. Tablets get their own layout with a side panel rather than a stretched phone screen.
