Gomoku

Five in a Row · 五子棋 · 五目並べ

Black and white stones on a 15×15 board. A minute to learn and a lifetime to master: the first player to make an unbroken line of five wins.

  • 2 players
  • vs AI or a friend
  • Works offline
  • Free

How to play Gomoku

  • Black places first, then the players take turns putting one stone on an empty intersection.
  • Stones never move and are never captured.
  • Five of your stones in a row, across, down or diagonally, wins the game.
  • Block your opponent's open threes and fours before they turn into five.
