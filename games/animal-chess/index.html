Animal Chess

Jungle · Dou Shou Qi · 斗兽棋

The childhood classic from China: eight animals a side on a 7×9 board with two rivers, traps and a den. The elephant rules the jungle, except that the tiny rat can bring it down.

  • 2 players
  • vs AI or a friend
  • Works offline
  • Free

How to play Animal Chess

  • From strongest to weakest: Elephant, Lion, Tiger, Leopard, Wolf, Dog, Cat, Rat.
  • Animals move one square up, down, left or right, and capture an enemy of equal or lower rank.
  • The Rat can capture the Elephant, but the Elephant cannot capture the Rat.
  • Only the Rat may swim. Lions and Tigers leap across the river, unless a Rat in the water blocks the way.
  • An enemy standing in one of your traps loses its rank, so any of your animals can take it.
  • Walk into the opponent's den, or capture every enemy animal, to win.

Animal Chess rules in full

The board

Animal Chess is played on a board seven squares wide and nine long. Two rivers, each two squares wide and three long, fill the middle. Each player has a den in the middle of their back row, with three traps around it.

Moving and capturing

  • Players take turns moving one animal one square up, down, left or right. Animals never move diagonally.
  • Move onto an enemy animal to capture it. An animal can capture an enemy of the same rank or lower.
  • The exception works both ways: the Rat can capture the Elephant, and the Elephant can never capture the Rat.
  • No animal may enter its own den.

Rivers

  • Only the Rat can go into the water.
  • A Rat in the water and an animal on land cannot capture each other. Two Rats in the water can.
  • The Lion and the Tiger jump over a river in a straight line, lengthwise or crosswise, to the bank on the other side, and may capture an enemy standing there.
  • Any Rat in the water along the way, yours or your opponent's, blocks the jump.

Traps and dens

  • An enemy animal standing on one of your traps loses its rank, so any of your animals can capture it: even your Cat can take their Lion.
  • Your own animals keep their full rank on your traps.
  • Move any animal into your opponent's den and you win at once.

Winning and draws

  • You win by entering the enemy den, by capturing every enemy animal, or when your opponent has no legal move.
  • If the same position comes up five times with the same player to move, or both players make thirty moves without a capture, the app offers a draw. Accept it, or play on and the count starts again. Games between two AIs are drawn automatically.

Tips for your first games

  • Guard your Rat: it is the only animal that can capture the enemy Elephant outside your traps.
  • A Rat waiting in the water stops the enemy Lion and Tiger from jumping that river.
  • An enemy that reaches your traps is easy to capture, so keep a defender near your den.
